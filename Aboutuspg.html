Why was this website made ?

In days of olde, when shadows long did creep, And weary souls o’er endless lists did weep, To find a tale of mirth or daring fight, Was but a quest that stole both day and night. From village hall to kingdom’s distant shore, The reels of motion left the seekers sore. ’Twas for this toil, this burden and this plight, I forged this humble lantern of delight: A guide to bring thee swiftly to the screen, Where dragons soar and noble hearts are seen.
